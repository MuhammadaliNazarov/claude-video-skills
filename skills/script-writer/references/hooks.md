# Hooks

A hook is not a sentence; it is three layers that must agree in the first second:
1. **Text hook** (on screen, read muted). Small, centred, upper-middle, sentence case, readable in under 2 s.
2. **Spoken hook** (if talking). The first words, no warm-up.
3. **Visual hook** (frame one): motion, a face mid-expression, an unexpected action (cleaning the camera, cutting a
   watermelon, kicking items away), or a striking place.

Kallaway's three moves (from his hook workshop summary): **context lean-in** (give just enough context that the viewer
leans in), **scroll-stop interjection** (a word, image or claim that breaks the pattern), **contrarian snapback** (flip the
expected take). Use at least two.

## Rules

- Open a gap the body closes: curiosity, stakes, contradiction, or identity ("this is so me").
- Call out one specific viewer ("If you're between 19-22, in uni, worried you're in the wrong major…").
- Prefer the creator's real numbers, age, place, and the exact words someone said to them.
- Keep proven hook wording when reusing a format; change the niche, not the mechanic ("the fewer
  variables we change, the more likely it performs").
- Write five, across different mechanisms below, and pick the one with the biggest gap and clearest payoff.
- Never bait-and-switch: the body must pay the hook in full.

## Level 1 first, then level 2 (Kallaway's Dopamine Ladder)

A hook has two jobs in order: **stimulate** (0-2 s: the eye stops on motion, colour, brightness or contrast, ~200 ms of
pre-conscious processing) and then **captivate** (open a high-stakes, relevant question). Write the first-frame visual
before the words: a moving shot, a face close to the lens, a bold red title on black, a sharp contrast. A great line
on a static, dark, empty frame loses at level 1. Extreme contrasts and unknowns make the best level-2 questions.

## Templates by mechanism (from proven formats + the Kallaway Reel)

**Curiosity / incomplete statement**
- "4 words that will change your life." → reveal
- "Life is better when…" → "When you do it for YOU." (reveal on the beat drop)
- "The harder you study…" → "the better your summer will feel" (unexpected effect)
- "Good things take time" → "I wish I had better skin" (before → after)
- "Some strange advice from the internet I laughed at until it worked"
- "Today is someone's last day on Earth" → "Do it scared. Do it alone. Do it broke. Do it anyway."

**Stakes / urgency**
- "Guys, I haven't been to my math class in 4 weeks and the midterm is in 3 hours"
- "[I have to X] if I don't [Y] in [short time]" (Kallaway stakes)

**Identity call-out**
- "If you're between [age], [situation], worrying [fear]…"
- "For all my [niche] folks: what's something you struggle with the most?"
- "Only [specific group] can relate"
- "Eldest daughters:" / "[niche] being told [thing]…"
- "I had everything a nice [identity] was supposed to be happy with."

**Quoted line / dialogue (someone said it to you)**
- "You need a jaw surgery"
- "Why do you work so hard? Who are you trying to impress?" → "Them" / "Her" (one-word answer)
- "U look happier, who healed u?" → "wdym 'who'?"
- "Aren't you afraid of doing things alone??" → a bigger counter-question
- "What's your motivation?" → "My past self." / "The future life I promised myself."
- "If you don't party, how do you have fun?"
- "Just use ChatGPT, it's easier" (then argue against it)
- "It's always 'my son is a doctor'"
- "She's way too positive. Bet she never struggles." (then the proof timeline)

**Contrarian / bold opinion**
- "All this social media shit don't matter. Make sure you're happy in real life."
- "[Your lifestyle] isn't Plan B. It's the plan."
- "3 reasons why [popular thing] isn't fun anymore (read caption)"
- "What people think is embarrassing:" vs "What is actually embarrassing:"
- "My toxic trait is [relatable thing]"
- "You can't [common behaviour] and then [contradiction]." (single-lesson story)

**List / number with authority**
- "Advice I'd give you as a [role] for [time] if I wasn't afraid of hurting your feelings"
- "[N] everyday habits you should quit now, from a [role]"
- "[N] things every [group] will regret not knowing"
- "I'm [age]… and I wish I [did X] in my 20s (read caption)"
- "[N] things that will keep you broke in your 20s, as a financially responsible [age]-year-old"
- "[N] foods you can literally overeat without getting fat"
- "#1 [topic] hack"
- "Steal my secrets to [outcome]" + the list on screen
- "If I had to [do X] again in my 20s, here's how I'd do it"
- "What [experience] taught me that university didn't"
- "What do I do as a [role]? (in 1 min)"
- "[N] signs you're [situation] (that I ignored)"
- "[N] stages of [life change]" / "[N] signs you've lived in [place] too long"
- "Things that are NORMAL as [niche noun]"
- "Things to do alone at least once in your life" (make it specific + numbered)

**Objection → answer**
- "'I want to travel the world but it's too expensive'" → "Welcome to my page, I got you ❤️"
- "'Will I be rich as a [role]?'" → reframes of rich
- "'I feel like I'm not making any progress'" → "You did your best / You didn't quit / …"

**Reframe pairs (negative thought → reframe)**
- "'I failed'" → "I'm proud that I tried" (repeat 4-6 pairs, end "Welcome to my page")
- "'You talk too much'" → "Start a podcast." (criticism → opportunity)
- "Be soft, but not weak." (virtue, but not its corrupted version)
- "I used to be their 10/10 daughter" → "9/10 when I said no…" (descending countdown)

**Visual / meme setups**
- "Dang, the camera is kinda dirty, let me clean it" (then a typographic message to music)
- "You vs. You" (ghost overlay of the productive you)
- "One bad month…" (grid of red dots) → "…is not a bad life" (one red dot among white)
- "'It's just 45 min'" shown twice: the lazy and the disciplined version
- "Letting fate pick my Saturday" (spin the bottle, kick away the wrong options)
- "POV: [blunt situation]" + running timestamps
- "This is [Name]" + a personality clip

**Kallaway-style explainer**
- "Here's how to [outcome] so [extreme result] it's [absurd claim]." → "This is called [named framework]. It's only
  [N] steps, but it uses the same [psychology] [familiar world] uses to [effect]."

## Captions (the post text) cheat sheet

- Repeat or complete the hook as the first line; "(read caption)" in the video moves long lists here.
- Long caption = the full story or the full list (numbered, emoji per number, 1-3 lines each).
- End with ONE: an engagement question ("Which one hurt the most? Drop the number"), a save prompt, a send prompt
  ("send this to a nurse who needs it"), a keyword CTA ("comment STORY and I'll send it"), or follow for part 2.
- Hashtags are optional; 3-5 niche tags when used.
