## The bookend (Gawx Art, "Perfect Imperfection.")

Open on a repeating pattern that sets up a missing piece, close on the same line with the piece complete.
"This is my cup collection. This is the one I made. This is my record collection. This is the one I made. And this is
my photo book collection… but I haven't made one of my own. Until now." → the film → "This is my photo book
collection. And this is the one I made." Between them: origin told through objects (one per era, numbered), a date
that creates stakes ("my flight is departing in 2 weeks"), a question ("but how do you even make a photo book?"),
the process, and the meaning said out loud ("there's a strange beauty in imperfection"). Works for any "I built /
made / learned X" story; for the user, e.g. "This is my laptop. This is my notebook. This is the code I wrote at 2 a.m.
in [city]… but I haven't built my own startup. Until now." Full breakdown:
`references/gawx-cinematic-doc.md`.
